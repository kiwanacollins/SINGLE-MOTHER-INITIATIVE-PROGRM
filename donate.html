Help A Mother Who Lost Everything Start Again

Single mothers who fled the war in Sudan arrive in Uganda carrying their children and little else. Your gift pays for food, hygiene supplies, counselling, training and childcare, the things that turn survival into a future.

3

Where Your Gift Goes

Four Programs That Rebuild A Family

You can support all programs or choose the one closest to your heart.

Emergency Assistance

Food, clothing, hygiene supplies, emergency financial support and referrals for safe accommodation.

Emotional Support

Peer-support groups, confidential counselling referrals and parenting support.

Economic Empowerment

Vocational training, language classes, financial literacy and small-business support.

Childcare & Children

Childcare, school enrolment, educational materials, health services and safe play.
