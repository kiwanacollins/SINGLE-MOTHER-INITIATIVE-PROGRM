About Us

Empowering Single Mothers To Rebuild Stable, Independent Lives

The Single Mothers Initiative Program (SMIP) is a community-based project designed to empower and support single mothers facing economic, social and family-related challenges.

SMIP provides targeted emergency assistance to vulnerable single mothers and their children during periods of crisis, and walks with them as they rebuild their lives. Founded in 2024, SMIP serves single mothers and vulnerable people, including mothers who have been displaced by war and whose homes and property have been destroyed.

How Help Works

From Crisis To Independence In Four Steps

1
Listen

We meet each mother with respect and learn what she and her children need most.

2
Respond

Food, clothing, hygiene supplies, emergency financial support and shelter referrals.

3
Heal

Peer-support groups, counselling referrals and parenting support.

4
Rebuild

Training, small-business support and childcare so she can stand on her own.

When Crisis Strikes

Urgent Needs We Respond To

Our emergency component is designed to reach vulnerable single mothers and their children quickly, meeting their most urgent needs so they can find safety and begin to recover.

  • Food insecurity
  • Medical emergencies
  • Temporary shelter
  • Essential household items
  • Child protection
  • Transportation
  • Limited financial assistance
The Sudan Crisis

Why Single Mothers From Sudan Need Us Now

Uganda hosts about two million refugees and asylum-seekers, and roughly 77% are women and children (UNHCR Uganda operational data). A mother who flees alone must protect, feed and shelter her children with no income and no home to return to.
