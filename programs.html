What We Do

Four Programs, One Goal: Stable And Independent Families

Program 1

Emergency Assistance

Food, clothing, hygiene supplies, emergency financial support and referrals for safe accommodation.

  • Food for mothers and children facing hunger
  • Clothing and essential household items
  • Hygiene supplies
  • Emergency financial support
  • Referrals for safe accommodation
Program 2

Psychosocial & Emotional Support

Peer-support groups, confidential counselling referrals and parenting support for mothers rebuilding after crisis.

  • Peer-support groups where mothers can share and heal together
  • Confidential counselling referrals
  • Parenting support
Program 3

Economic Empowerment

Vocational training, language classes, job-readiness workshops, financial literacy and small-business support.

  • Vocational training
  • Language classes
  • Job-readiness workshops
  • Financial literacy sessions
  • Small-business development support
Program 4

Childcare & Children's Development

Connecting mothers with childcare, school enrolment, educational materials, health services and safe play.

  • Access to childcare
  • School enrolment
  • Educational materials
  • Health services
  • Safe recreational activities
Get Involved

Together We Can Give Every Mother A New Beginning

Many of the mothers we meet have fled conflict with nothing but their children. SMIP was founded in 2024 and is only at the beginning of its journey. Your partnership, time and support can help us reach single mothers at their most difficult moments.

How You Can Help

  • Partner with us as an organisation
  • Volunteer your time and skills
  • Support our emergency assistance
  • Speak up and share the story of single mothers
