Who We Are

A Community-Based Program For Single Mothers And Vulnerable Families

The Single Mothers Initiative Program (SMIP) is a community-based project designed to empower and support single mothers facing economic, social and family-related challenges.

SMIP provides targeted emergency assistance to vulnerable single mothers and their children during periods of crisis, and walks with them as they rebuild their lives. Founded in 2024, SMIP serves single mothers and vulnerable people, including mothers who have been displaced by war and whose homes and property have been destroyed.

Mission and Vision

Why We Exist

Our Mission

To provide timely emergency support, protection, and empowerment to single mothers and their children during crisis, helping them meet basic needs, regain stability, and build resilient, independent lives.

Our Vision

A society where every single mother and her children live with dignity, security, opportunity and hope for a better future.

Our Approach

How We Work

Four principles shape every decision, from the first conversation with a mother to the last report to a donor.

Mothers Lead

We listen first. Each mother tells us what her family needs and takes part in the plan.

Privacy & Consent

Names, faces and stories are shared only with a mother's permission.

Child Safeguarding

The safety and wellbeing of children come before everything else.

Honest Giving

Every gift is acknowledged and used for the purpose the donor chooses.

Core Values

The Values That Guide Everything We Do

Dignity

We treat every mother and child with respect and honour their worth.

Empowerment

We equip mothers with the skills and confidence to stand on their own.

Compassion

We respond to every need with kindness, patience and care.

Integrity

We are honest, transparent and accountable in all we do.

Equality

Every mother deserves equal access to support and opportunity.

Resilience

We help families recover from crisis and grow stronger.

Protection

We put the safety and wellbeing of mothers and children first.

Emergency Component

Support When Mothers Need It Most

SMIP provides targeted emergency assistance to vulnerable single mothers and their children during periods of crisis. The emergency component addresses urgent needs such as:

  • Food insecurity
  • Medical emergencies
  • Temporary shelter
  • Essential household items
  • Child protection
  • Transportation
  • Limited financial assistance

Who Can Help Fund This Work

SMIP welcomes support from organisations and individuals who share our goal of dignity and independence for single mothers.

Local & International NGOs

Joint programs, shared resources and referrals for mothers in crisis.

Women's Empowerment Organisations

Training, mentoring and networks that help mothers build independent livelihoods.

Corporate Social Responsibility

Company giving, staff volunteering and in-kind donations of goods and services.

Government Social Development Departments

Coordination on social protection, child welfare and support services for vulnerable families.

Churches & Community Organisations

Trusted local networks that reach mothers and mobilise neighbours to help.

International Development Foundations

Grants that help us grow our programs and reach more mothers and children.

Individual Philanthropists

Personal gifts, large or small, that give a mother food, safety and a fresh start.
