Who We Are

A Community-Based Program For Single Mothers And Vulnerable Families

The Single Mothers Initiative Program (SMIP) is a community-based project designed to empower and support single mothers facing economic, social and family-related challenges.

SMIP provides targeted emergency assistance to vulnerable single mothers and their children during periods of crisis, and walks with them as they rebuild their lives. Founded in 2024, SMIP serves single mothers and vulnerable people, including mothers who have been displaced by war and whose homes and property have been destroyed.

Mission and Vision

Why We Exist

Our Mission

To provide timely emergency support, protection, and empowerment to single mothers and their children during crisis, helping them meet basic needs, regain stability, and build resilient, independent lives.

Our Vision

A society where every single mother and her children live with dignity, security, opportunity and hope for a better future.

Core Values

The Values That Guide Everything We Do

Dignity

We treat every mother and child with respect and honour their worth.

Empowerment

We equip mothers with the skills and confidence to stand on their own.

Compassion

We respond to every need with kindness, patience and care.

Integrity

We are honest, transparent and accountable in all we do.

Equality

Every mother deserves equal access to support and opportunity.

Resilience

We help families recover from crisis and grow stronger.

Protection

We put the safety and wellbeing of mothers and children first.

Emergency Component

Support When Mothers Need It Most

SMIP provides targeted emergency assistance to vulnerable single mothers and their children during periods of crisis. The emergency component addresses urgent needs such as:

  • Food insecurity
  • Medical emergencies
  • Temporary shelter
  • Essential household items
  • Child protection
  • Transportation
  • Limited financial assistance
